# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probation are eligible for hybrid work. Employees on probation work from the office at least 4 days a week.

## Hybrid Schedule

The standard hybrid schedule is 3 days in office and 2 days remote per week. Teams may set common in-office anchor days.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from both the department head and HR, and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Equipment and Allowance

The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 and a monthly internet reimbursement of up to INR 1,500.

## Working from Another Location

Working from a different city for more than 2 consecutive weeks needs prior manager approval. Working from outside India is not permitted without written approval from HR and Legal, because of tax and data-protection obligations.

## Security

Remote work must be done over the company VPN. Confidential data must not be stored on personal devices.
