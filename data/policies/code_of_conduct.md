# Code of Conduct

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, freelance work or business interest that could conflict with their role. Disclosures are submitted to HR through the HR portal and reviewed by the ethics committee.
Freelance or part-time work for a competitor or client of the company is not permitted.

## Gifts

Employees may accept gifts from vendors or clients worth up to INR 2,000. Gifts above this value must be declared to the ethics committee and may need to be returned.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns about misconduct can be reported to a manager, to HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. The company does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Data Protection

Candidate and employee personal data may only be accessed for a legitimate business purpose and must not be shared outside the company without approval from Legal.
