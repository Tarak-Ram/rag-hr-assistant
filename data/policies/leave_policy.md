# Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time employees of Nimbus Works (a fictional company used for this demo).

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of annual leave per completed month of service, which is 18 days per calendar year.
Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Days beyond 10 lapse on 31 December.
Annual leave requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 14 days in advance.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
Adoptive parents of a child under 3 months receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 floating holidays per year. Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year.

## How to Apply

Leave is applied for in the HR portal under Leave > Apply. Requests are approved by the reporting manager. If the manager does not respond within 3 working days, the request is escalated to the HR business partner.
