# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One

New joiners receive their offer letter, background-verification form and pre-boarding checklist through the HR portal. Background verification must be completed before the joining date.

## Day One

On day one, new joiners collect their laptop and access card from the IT desk and attend a 2-hour orientation session run by HR.

## First 30 Days

Each new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 30 days. Mandatory trainings on information security, code of conduct and prevention of sexual harassment (POSH) must be completed within 30 days of joining.

## Probation

The probation period is 90 days. A probation review with the reporting manager happens in the last 2 weeks of probation. Probation can be extended once, by up to 60 days.

## Notice Period

During probation the notice period is 15 days. After confirmation the notice period is 60 days.
