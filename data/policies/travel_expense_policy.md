# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days in advance, except for emergencies approved by a director.
Domestic flights are booked in economy class. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy.

## Daily Allowance

The daily allowance for domestic travel is INR 2,500 for meals and local transport. For international travel the daily allowance is USD 75.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night in other cities.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the HR portal within 30 days of returning from the trip, with itemised receipts for every expense above INR 500.
Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

## Non-reimbursable Expenses

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and upgrades not approved in advance are not reimbursed.
